# Content

This folder contains internalized content seeds for the Internal Dev Platform.

## Files

- `reference-sites-internalization.md`: structure and taxonomy extracted from the two reference sites, rewritten as a Korean internal-app content model.
- `internal-app-content-seed.json`: product-ready seed content for sample apps, components, workflow terms, and copyable AI prompts.
- `vibecoding-glossary-84.html`: 84 UI/vibe-coding glossary terms rewritten as a Korean internal-app glossary with click-through detail panels.
- `ui-design-dictionary-108.html`: 108 visual UI patterns rewritten as a Korean internal-business-app pattern dictionary with searchable cards and detail panels.

## Content Policy

The reference sites are used as product inspiration, not as source copy. Keep future entries in this folder:

- Korean-first.
- Focused on internal business apps.
- Written in our own wording.
- Paired with visual intent, usage context, and an AI prompt.
- Connected to a concrete action such as "create screen", "copy prompt", or "start from template".

Avoid storing raw mirrored site copy here. If raw captures are needed for temporary analysis, keep them out of product content files.
